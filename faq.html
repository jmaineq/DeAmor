  • How can I order flowers from you?

    Ordering is easy:

    1. Find a flower that you like, by clicking on the product catalog, and choose flowers.
    2. Pick a flower then click the "Order Now!" button.
    3. Once in the order form page, fill-out all the necessary information including your name, the delivery address and your contact number.
    4. Choose your preferred payment method, whether it is GCash, Mastercard or Cash on delivery.
    5. Click "Submit order".

    Similarly you can order hanging baskets with Step 1 and choose "Baskets".
    You can also order gifts by choosing "Gifts".

  • How do I know if my order is confirmed?

    You will receive an order confirmation text message at the contact number provided to us.
    It will be an automatically generated message, so do check your spam folder if you do not see the message in your inbox.

    No, unfortunately not. We deliver 100% of our orders and do not operate a storefront business.
